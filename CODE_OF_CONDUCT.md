# Code Of Conduct

## Standard

LNSAT contributors should keep collaboration professional, direct, and
evidence-based. Technical disagreement is expected; personal attacks,
harassment, doxxing, hate speech, and repeated bad-faith disruption are not.

## Scope

This applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions,
documentation, release coordination, and future community spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may warn, moderate, close, block, or ban behavior that undermines
project safety or productive work. Security-sensitive moderation should avoid
publicly exposing vulnerability details or private reporter information.

When practical, a conflict-free maintainer reviews a report, gathers only the
information needed to understand it, and communicates the outcome privately.
Possible responses include no action, clarification, warning, content removal,
temporary restriction, or permanent restriction. Decisions should consider
severity, pattern, risk to participants, and available evidence rather than
technical disagreement alone.

## Reporting

Repository does not yet publish a dedicated conduct-reporting address. Until one
exists, use the
[community support form](https://github.com/hypler-dev/LNSAT/issues/new?template=community_support.yml)
to request private maintainer contact using neutral wording. Select `Private
conduct-contact request` and do not include incident details, names, screenshots,
or other sensitive information in the public request.

A private report may identify what happened, where it occurred, relevant dates,
available public links, requested safety measures, and any reviewer conflict.
Maintainers should limit disclosure to people needed for review and avoid
retaliation against good-faith reporters. Pre-release community handling is
best-effort; no response-time or resolution-time promise exists.

Suspected software vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not the
conduct-contact path.
