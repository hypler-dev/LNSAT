# Maintainers

## Current Maintainer

- Jeff / Hypler

## Maintainer Responsibilities

- Own roadmap and release sequencing.
- Preserve product-source and marketing-source separation.
- Review security-sensitive changes.
- Review release trust evidence before promotion.
- Keep source-only and live-scope boundaries explicit.
- Maintain public roadmap, release notes, and governance docs.

## Future Maintainer Path

Future maintainers should be added after repeated accepted contributions,
demonstrated judgment on policy/audit/security boundaries, and explicit approval
from existing maintainers.

## Sensitive Authority

Maintainer status does not by itself grant authority to mutate live
infrastructure, publish releases or packages, access secrets, or handle customer
data. Those actions require explicit scope, review, and approval.
